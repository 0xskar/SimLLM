# SimLLM Concent

- LLM agents living in a small city of 100. this will be customizable in the settings for the sim.

- each agent has their own memories and personalities. they need to get along with other and grow and live together and make a community or civilization.

- each generation of the sim has its own save so you can go in and out and pickup where you left off

- they need an economy so they are suggested to work together

- they can form friendships and do anything, its basically a sim for LLM agents

- runs locally off of ollama, user should be able to pick loade dmodel to use from settings

- each llm should be represented by emoji of their choice from their personality. these emojis can change based on attitude.

- there needs to be logs for debugging.

- they should be able to build houses and basically do whatever humans can do in a virtual enviroment within limits.

- you should be able to click on each agent and see what and how they are doing and see their stats, like money,  and their conversations, and history
